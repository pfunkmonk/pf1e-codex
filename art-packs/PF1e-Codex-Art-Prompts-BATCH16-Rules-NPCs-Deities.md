# PF1e Codex — Art Prompts — BATCH 16 (Rules, NPCs, and the last of the pantheon)

**233 images.**

The rules bucket is 3,102 pages on 40 scenes. This takes it to 181. NPCs go 12 to 48. And the
remaining 163 deities finish that bucket at 463 of 463 — which also gives the 57 religion traits
whose deity had no art a picture of their own god.

## How to use this

1. Generate one image per prompt below, in order.
2. **Save each file using the exact _filename_ given in bold.** The site looks images up by that
   name; a renamed file is an invisible file.
3. Render at **1280x720** (16:9). Larger is fine and will be downscaled on ingest; smaller is not.
4. If the generator produces two candidates per prompt, keep both — the ingest step picks one and
   the spare stays available as an alternate.
5. Drop everything into the `CODEX IMAGES` folder. Nothing else is needed.
